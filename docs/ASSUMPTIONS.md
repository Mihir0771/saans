# Assumptions & Parameter Sources (`docs/ASSUMPTIONS.md`)

Every constant in [`src/engine/params.ts`](../src/engine/params.ts) carries a central value, low/high uncertainty range, unit, source note, and confidence grade:

- **A**: Published primary guideline, statutory standard, or peer-reviewed handbook value.
- **B**: Published empirical range from peer-reviewed or institutional transport/indoor-air studies.
- **C**: Conservative engineering heuristic where exact local measurements vary widely (explicitly labelled in the UI "Show the math" drawer).

## Known Limitations

1. Atmospheric forecasts from Open-Meteo / CAMS global (~25–45 km grid) smooth out hyperlocal road and neighbourhood hotspots. Users can override with a local monitor via **Calibrate**.
2. Well-mixed single-zone indoor physics treats the target room as uniform; real multi-room mixing (`η_mix = 0.70–0.95`) varies with door openness and ceiling fans.
3. Dose estimates (`µg` PM2.5 and `× WHO guideline`) are exposure estimates, **never** predictions of individual illness or medical outcomes.
