# Saans (सांस) — 2-Minute Demo Video Script

Target runtime: **115 seconds** at crisp pacing (1280×800 or split desktop/mobile view).

---

## 0:00 – 0:18 · The Problem & Breath Budget Hero (`Today`, `?demo=sharma`)

- **Visual:** Start on `/?demo=sharma` (`Dwarka, Delhi · 150 µg/m³ · Demo` banner clearly visible). Show Aarav's Breath Budget gauge (`typical` vs `with your Saans plan`, expressed as multiples of the WHO 24-hour PM2.5 guideline).
- **Narration:** "Most air apps stop at AQI 323 — scary, but silent on what a family should actually do. Saans turns live hourly pollution into a **personal Breath Budget** per family member. Here is the Sharma household on a Delhi winter smog day."
- **Click:** Switch from `Aarav` to `Dadi`. Point out how Dadi's dose multiple (`6.4× -> 2.5×`) updates dynamically because breathing rate and schedule differ per person. Click **"Show the math"** to open the transparent equation modal, then close it.

## 0:18 – 0:45 · Plan ("Your Day, Two Ways") & Marginal Waterfall

- **Visual:** Click **Plan** tab. Show cumulative dose curves (dashed typical day vs solid Saans plan) and click **"View as table"** briefly. Toggle **"DIY Corsi-Rosenthal filter"** or **"Metro commute"** off and back on.
- **Narration:** "On Plan, two curves show PM2.5 dose accumulating hour by hour. Below, an attributed waterfall proves what each habit is worth in rupee cost and dose saved — toggling any switch recomputes the entire day immediately."

## 0:45 – 1:10 · Home Air Lab, Smart Ventilation & Snap-and-Confirm

- **Visual:** Click **Home Lab** tab. Toggle leakiness (`leaky` -> `average`), scrub purifier CADR, point to the low/central/high uncertainty band, Smart Ventilation Window card, and `₹1,500–2,800` DIY Corsi-Rosenthal BOM with CO safety note. Briefly show **Snap & Confirm** photo upload confirmation UI.
- **Narration:** "Inside Home Lab, a physical mass-balance model computes indoor PM2.5 with honest uncertainty bands and finds the best 20-minute window to air out — or warns when keeping windows shut is safer."

## 1:10 – 1:35 · Grounded Gemini Ask Saans + Language & Theme Flip

- **Visual:** Click **Ask** tab. Click a suggested question chip (`"Is a purifier worth it for one room?"`). Highlight the **"Numbers verified by engine"** / **"Offline guidance"** trust badge and **"Show the math"** button on the reply. Toggle **`हिं` (Hindi)** and **`☾/☀` Dark mode**.
- **Narration:** "Ask Saans never lets Gemini invent a number: the engine computes a signed FactSheet, Gemini writes plain English or Devanagari using `{{fact:id}}` slots, and our server guard blocks unverified digits, ozone generators, and child-mask hazards."

## 1:35 – 1:55 · Community Kit & Proof Transparency

- **Visual:** Open **Community** (`Dwarka Public School · 250 headcount`, hourly go/no-go table, WhatsApp share button) then **Proof** tab (assumptions, WHO/CPCB/EPA citations, Myth-or-Fact cards).
- **Narration:** "Schools and RWAs get printable hour-by-hour activity schedules, and every assumption carries a real source and confidence grade on Proof. Saans: less guesswork, cleaner breaths."
